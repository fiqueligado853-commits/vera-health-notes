If heavy legs keep making stairs, errands, or standing to cook harder after work, write down what changed and when. A short note about the shift, the symptom, and what it kept you from doing gives your healthcare team a starting point. Start with safety before keeping a log.

## Know when the situation is urgent

> **When to get help:** Seek urgent medical evaluation for new unexplained swelling in one leg, even without pain. Pain, warmth or a change in skin color are other signs to report. Call 911 for sudden trouble breathing or chest pain, even if you have no leg symptoms. [The NHLBI describes these signs of a possible blood clot](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/venous-thromboembolism/symptoms).

## Is it heaviness, pain, stiffness, or weakness?

Those words are often used interchangeably in conversation, but they can describe different experiences. Stiffness after activity, aching around the calves, a heavy sensation, and true muscle weakness are not identical symptoms. If you contact a clinician, explain what you mean in your own words. Is walking difficult? Does the sensation affect balance? Is it present when sitting or only after standing? Clear descriptions are more useful than an internet diagnosis.

## Write down three details after a shift

| Detail | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Shift | The time, whether you were mostly standing or sitting, and any unusual activity |
| Symptom | The side and location, what it felt like, and any visible swelling or color change |
| Effect | Which task changed, such as walking upstairs, and whether the feeling eased |

A sample entry might read: “Friday, 6:15 p.m., after standing most of the shift. Both calves felt heavy. Going upstairs was harder than usual. No visible swelling.” Record a few ordinary details if symptoms recur while you’re arranging care. If a warning sign appears, seek care right away.

## Bring the record to your visit

Mention any recent medication changes and bring an up-to-date list. Ask which changes should prompt earlier care and whether you should adjust your activities while the cause is being assessed. The record is a way to explain what happened during your day.
