Before you measure

  • Follow your healthcare professional's monitoring instructions.
  • Sit quietly for at least five minutes before taking a reading.
  • Keep your back supported, feet flat, and arm at heart level.
  • Use a correctly fitting cuff on bare skin.
  • Take two readings one minute apart and record both.
